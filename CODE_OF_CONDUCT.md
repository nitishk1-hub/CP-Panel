# Community expectations

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks, harassment, threats or discriminatory conduct. Keep reports relevant, constructive and free of private credentials or personal information.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content and restrict participation to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer using the repository's available private contact channel. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers must publish a usable contact route when opening the project to community contributions.
